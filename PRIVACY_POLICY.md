# Privacy Policy for KPI Tracker

**Last updated: November 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how KPI Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our browser extension.

## Data Collection and Usage

**We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of your personal data.**

Everything you log in KPI Tracker (achievements, categories, timestamps and display preferences) is stored locally on your own device using the Chrome Storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). The extension makes no network requests: your data is never sent to any server, and there are no analytics or tracking scripts.

## Exports and Backups

When you export a report (PDF, CSV or Markdown), copy a report to the clipboard, or download a backup, the file is created on your device and saved wherever you choose. Restoring a backup reads only the file you select.

## Permissions

- **storage**: used solely to save your entries and preferences on your device.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it is as secure as your personal computer and browser. We have no access to your data. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the author via the GitHub repository.

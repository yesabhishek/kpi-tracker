# Privacy Policy for KPI Tracker

**Last updated: January 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how KPI Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our browser extension.

## Data Collection and Usage

**We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of your personal data.**

Everything you log in KPI Tracker (achievements, categories, timestamps) is stored locally on your own device using the Chrome Storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your computer and is not sent to any external servers.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it is as secure as your personal computer and browser. We have no access to your data.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the author via the GitHub repository.
